# obsidian-second-brain

## For future agent
This note describes obsidian-second-brain, a tool Company Brain depends on. obsidian-second-brain (https://github.com/eugeniughelbur/obsidian-second-brain, MIT) is the single-person AI second-brain toolkit Company Brain is built on. Company Brain vendors its AI-first rules, write validator, and vault MCP server, and adds team and company layers. It can keep running on a personal vault alongside Company Brain.

Some Company Brain views are built from upstream files at run time, so upstream improvements flow in: `Company/Org/People.base` is generated by `brain refresh` from upstream's `people.base.template`, vendored byte for byte in `.company-brain/references/bases/`, with Company Brain's folders and columns laid over it. To take a newer upstream version: `./brain upstream-check --copy`, then `./brain refresh`.
